Corporations


"If you don't drive your business, you will be driven out of business."
- B.C. Forbes
Trade and commerce has been a focal point of civilization since ancient times. Corporations are modern entities that authorizes a company or single group to act as a single entity. Corporations have become the largest facilitators of trade and influence in today's society. In Civilization V, Corporations allow you cement your civilization's wealth and deliver powerful trade benefits to your civilization.

To establish a Corporation, you must secure a Global Monopoly on any of its required resources, and found its Corporation Headquarters. After your Corporation is established, you may construct Offices in your cities and use them to build Franchises in foreign cities via :c5trade: Trade Routes.

Centaurus Extractors, Inc.

Game Info:

Supports the Cultural and Spaceship Victories by generating large amounts of yields in coastal Cities.
• +1 :c5trade: Trade Route.
• +100% Sea Trade Route Range.
• +8 :c5production: Production from Mine Field in all Cities.
• +1 :tourism: Tourism from all Specialists.
• Every Franchise in a foreign Civilization's Cities increases :tourism: Tourism with them by 2%.

Resource Bonus:

Pearls, Whales, Crab, and Coral worked by the City gain +5 :c5production: Production from Centaurus Extractors Offices and Franchises.

Office Bonus:

+1 :c5production: Production for every Global Franchise. +1 :c5production: Production and :c5culture: Culture on all Sea Tiles near the City and a further +1 :c5science: Science and :c5culture: Culture on Sea Resources.

Trade Route Bonus:

:c5trade: Trade Routes from Cities with a Centaurus Extractors Office to Cities with a Centaurus Extractors Franchise grant +3% :c5production: Production to the origin City.

Monopoly Required (Any):

  • Whales
  • Crab
  • Coral
  • Pearls
